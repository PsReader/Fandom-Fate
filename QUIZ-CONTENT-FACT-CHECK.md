# Expanded Fandom Quiz Content — Fact-Check Notes

## Scope

The expanded sets are **personality-match quizzes**, not trivia quizzes. The player-facing questions are original scenarios and values prompts, so they do not assert plot facts or require a single canon answer. The outcome names and short descriptions use broad, recognizable characterization from the named source franchises without reproducing dialogue or source text.

## Content safeguards

- No answer asks the player to identify a factual event, relationship, power, title, or quotation.
- No official quotes, logos, screenshots, or character artwork were added.
- Descriptions avoid asserting disputed continuity details, adaptations, deaths, romances, or timeline claims.
- Franchise names and character names are used only to identify the fan-made quiz topic and result.
- The site disclaimer remains in place: Fandom Fate is unofficial fan content and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any rights holder.

## Bonus-question additions

Harry Potter Character Match, Harry Potter House Sorting, Marvel, Star Wars, and Lord of the Rings each now have two additional, explicitly labeled **bonus** questions. These are harder ethical and leadership dilemmas rather than trivia: they ask about agency, accountability, legacy, tradition, coalition-building, and the cost of power. They remain original scenario prompts and do not depend on a player knowing a particular plot event.

The Harry Potter, Marvel, Star Wars, and Lord of the Rings result descriptions were also expanded to explain the more nuanced interpretation behind each match. The added language stays at the level of broad characterization and avoids claims about disputed continuity or adaptation-specific details.

## Secondary quiz formats

The catalog now gives every fandom at least two playable, independent quiz formats. The added quizzes use original scenario prompts rather than trivia and keep their outcome sets distinct from the existing character-match entries:

- Harry Potter: Spellcraft Style
- The Lord of the Rings: Fellowship Role
- Lord of the Mysteries: Mystery Role
- Marvel: Team Role
- Disney: Story Role
- Star Wars: Force Compass
- Stranger Things: Survival Role
- The Hunger Games: Resistance Role

Each added set contains eight questions, four outcomes, balanced answer coverage, and no quotations, official artwork, or plot-dependent fact claims.

## Advanced question round

Every playable quiz now ends with two original **deep-dive** questions. These are complex, lore-aware dilemmas rather than objective trivia with a single canon answer: they reference the tone, stakes, and recognizable themes of each fandom while preserving the personality-match scoring model. Each new question includes one answer mapped to every outcome in its quiz, so the added round cannot make any result unreachable.

The new prompts cover agency, power, legacy, accountability, public narratives, difficult alliances, and the cost of choosing a shortcut. They avoid quotations, official artwork, and adaptation-specific claims.

## Data validation checklist

The expanded data was checked for:

- Valid JavaScript syntax.
- One catalog entry and one definition for each playable quiz ID.
- The authored question count for every quiz, including bonus rounds and the two-question deep-dive ending on every quiz.
- The authored outcome count for every quiz.
- Every answer mapping resolves to an outcome in the same quiz.
- Each outcome is used by at least one answer, with balanced coverage (each outcome is mapped in every question, and no answer points outside its quiz). Harry Potter Character Match and House Sorting, Marvel, Star Wars, and Lord of the Rings have ten questions; Disney, Stranger Things, and The Hunger Games have eight.
- The existing quiz runtime remains unchanged except for preserving the active quiz when the player selects **Try Again**.
