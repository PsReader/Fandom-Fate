# Fandom Fate — Visual Direction

## Direction
A handmade fan-club zine, not a glossy app template: warm paper, ink rules, expressive serif headlines, and a small original open-book illustration. Keep it distinctive without stacking stickers, stamps, and labels everywhere.

## Layout and rhythm
- Give each view one clear job and a strong headline.
- The homepage has one primary action: browse the fandom list. The cover illustration supplies visual character without extra hero callouts.
- Use generous section breaks, tighter card interiors, and a responsive single-column mobile hero.
- Fandom cards share a quiet structure: title, short description, and availability. Only playable quizzes get an action button; coming-soon entries should not mimic disabled buttons.
- As the catalog grows, treat each fandom as a hub that can list several distinct quiz types—such as a character match and house sort—with independent status labels. Keep direct launch for a fandom that still has only one playable quiz.
- Keep the quiz focused on the question, progress bar, and readable answer controls. Keep the result focused on the match, traits, share, and retake actions.

## Palette and type
Warm paper `#F5F0E4`, ink `#292638`, brick red `#C95541`, slate blue `#536889`, ochre `#DDB653`, and dusty rose `#C7828A`. Reserve stronger color for the playable card and primary actions; keep upcoming cards neutral. Use a Georgia-style editorial serif for headlines, Trebuchet/Arial for body copy, and Courier New for small labels. Use system fonts only.

## Signature details
Use the open-book brand mark, bookmark tab, simple book illustration, paper-plane detail, and one issue label. Prefer ruled separators and small typographic marks over repeated star or question symbols. Use modest shadows and minimal motion; preserve visible keyboard focus and respect reduced-motion settings.

## Brand voice and assets
Fan-to-fan, warm, lightly teasing, and specific. Keep the unofficial-fan disclaimer visible and do not imply franchise affiliation. Use original inline SVG and original copy only—no external images, copied quotes, official logos, or remote fonts.
