# Lord of the Mysteries — Pathway Sorting Quiz Draft

**Status:** Playable draft. This is an original fan-made content draft for the next Fandom Fate entry. It is now wired into the local gameplay engine and can be launched from the Lord of the Mysteries card.

## 1. Concept

**Working title:** Which Lord of the Mysteries Pathway fits you?

This quiz sorts a player toward a closest-fit Pathway based on how they handle uncertainty, power, responsibility, risk, and identity. The questions are written as values-and-instinct prompts rather than trivia, so a player does not need to remember every Beyonder sequence to participate.

The first playable version should use a focused ten-Pathway set. It is intentionally a starter scope rather than a claim that the wider Lord of the Mysteries cosmology has only ten choices. Additional Pathways can be added later as separate outcomes once each has enough distinct question coverage.

- **Audience note:** Unofficial fan content; expect setting spoilers and terminology from the series.
- **Voice:** Mysterious, playful, and readable rather than grim for its own sake.
- **Rights note:** Use original descriptions and abstract motifs only. Do not use official art, logos, or copied source-work passages.

## 2. Starter outcome set

| Pathway | Core fit | Result voice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fool | Adaptability, disguise, possibility | You keep several versions of yourself ready and can make uncertainty useful. Your advantage is knowing that a rigid plan is not the same thing as control. |
| Door | Curiosity, travel, access | You are drawn to thresholds, routes, and the answer on the other side. You learn by going farther—and by remembering how to return. |
| Error | Improvisation, loopholes, clever disruption | You notice the small inconsistency that changes the whole situation. Rules are information; the interesting question is where they leave room. |
| Visionary | Imagination, narrative, transformation | You can see what a person or situation might become before it is obvious to anyone else. Your ideas change the shape of the room. |
| Sun | Clarity, conviction, protection | You bring light to confusion and prefer a truth that can be acted on. People trust you to make the important thing visible. |
| Tyrant | Force, command, decisive momentum | When a storm arrives, you would rather take the helm than debate the weather. You turn intensity into movement and protect your crew by acting. |
| White Tower | Knowledge, analysis, precision | You want to understand the mechanism before you touch the lever. Careful observation is not hesitation when it prevents a larger mistake. |
| Hanged Man | Sacrifice, patience, hidden strength | You can endure the uncomfortable part long enough to learn what it is asking of you. You see value in restraint, depth, and difficult perspective. |
| Demoness | Self-knowledge, social perception, metamorphosis | You read the emotional weather and understand that identity is never as fixed as it pretends to be. Your power is noticing what others avoid naming. |
| Red Priest | Courage, challenge, catalytic change | You come alive when a stagnant situation needs heat. You are willing to enter the conflict, change the terms, and make a new route through it. |

## 3. Player-facing questions

The Pathway mapping is hidden from players. Each question has five reasonable answers; the answer positions rotate so no outcome is always associated with the same letter.

| D | Find the loophole or overlooked detail that can still make the plan work. | Error |

## 4. Scoring and tie handling

1. Start every Pathway at zero.
2. Add one point to the hidden Pathway mapped to each selected answer.
3. The highest score is the result when there is one clear leader.
4. If two or more Pathways tie for the highest score, show one additional tie-break question containing only those tied Pathways. Do not add the tie-break answer to the main score.
5. If the tie-break itself somehow cannot resolve the result, use the authored outcome order above as the final deterministic fallback.

### Tie-break question

**When the future becomes difficult to read, what would you most want people to count on you for?**

- Keeping several possible selves and routes alive — **Fool**
- Finding the way through a boundary — **Door**
- Seeing the flaw that changes the problem — **Error**
- Imagining the change nobody else can see yet — **Visionary**
- Making the truth clear enough to protect people — **Sun**
- Taking the helm when hesitation becomes dangerous — **Tyrant**
- Understanding the mechanism before acting — **White Tower**
- Enduring the hard perspective long enough to learn from it — **Hanged Man**
- Recognizing the hidden feeling or identity in the room — **Demoness**
- Turning conflict into the beginning of a new order — **Red Priest**

The implemented tie-break should preserve the neutral order of only the tied choices, while retaining the same hidden mappings.

## 5. Result-card copy direction

Use the result framing **“Your Pathway is…”** rather than “You are a Beyonder.” Present the outcome as a playful closest fit, not a canon identity or power recommendation. Each result should include:

- Pathway name
- One-sentence core fit from the outcome table
- Three or four original traits
- A small abstract motif (threshold, sunburst, keyhole, eye, compass, wave, or flame) rather than official character or faction artwork
- A visible unofficial-fan-content disclaimer

## 6. Implementation notes

The Lord of the Mysteries fandom card is marked `live`, and its **Start quiz** action passes that quiz definition into the shared runtime. The current Harry Potter character-match quiz remains unchanged.
